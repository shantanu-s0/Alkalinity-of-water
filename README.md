# Alkalinity-of-water
Determined the alkalinity of water samples from campus sources by using phenolphthalein and methyl orange indicators and HCL as titrant , Water Alkalinity measure the sample's ability to neutralize acids. Alkalinity is governed by three primary ionic species: Hydroxide (OH⁻), Carbonate (CO₃²⁻), and Bicarbonate (HCO₃⁻) Determining Alkalinity is governed by three primary ionic species: Hydroxide (OH⁻), Carbonate (CO₃²⁻), and Bicarbonate (HCO₃⁻) Determining Alkalinity is critical for protecting aquatic ecosystems, preventing pipe corrosion or scaling in industrial systems, and ensuring effective municipal water and wastewater treatment.
